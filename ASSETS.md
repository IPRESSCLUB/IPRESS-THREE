# Artwork and typography

## Identity

The IPRESS logo is user supplied. Its original file is preserved as `assets/ipress-logo-original.png`. The visible logo pixels in `assets/ipress-logo.png` are identical. The logo was not generated.

## Clay camera

Generated with the built-in Imagegen tool, then resized and encoded as WebP for the page. Transparency is preserved. No external image URLs are needed at runtime.

- Website asset: `assets/clay-camera.webp`, 608 × 608.
- Full-resolution source: `assets/clay-camera-source.png`, 1254 × 1254.
- The illustration is decorative and has no embedded IPRESS branding.

Original generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent decorative website asset for IPRESS, a youthful photography and journalism club. Create a beautiful boutique claymorphism 3D still life of one chunky soft cobalt-blue compact camera with a very thick raised midnight-blue lens, an ivory-white lens highlight, a small leafy-green shutter button, and a tiny white sculpted star hovering near it. One folded white miniature newspaper peeks behind the camera on its left, with a few blue abstract horizontal lines, absolutely NO text. The camera is the hero, almost front-facing but turned 12 degrees in three-quarter perspective, cheerfully tilted counterclockwise 10 degrees. Exquisite inflated marshmallow/silicone shapes, pillowy rounded edges, matte polymer-clay surface, tactile handmade details, subtle realistic ambient occlusion and soft studio light from upper left. Restrict every part to white, true blue (from light sky blue through rich cobalt to midnight navy), and leaf green. Do not introduce purple, cyan, pink, orange, red, yellow, grey or black. Compact, isolated group fills the square canvas with clean generous padding. Truly transparent background, no backdrop, no checkerboard, no ground plane, no floor shadow extending far from the object. No lettering, no logos, no watermarks. Polished premium 3D illustration, not cartoon outlines, not photorealistic camera hardware. The PNG will be shown at 250px wide as a floating decoration; prioritize a bold silhouette and easily readable materials.

## Other visual elements

The logo cushion, speech bubbles, globe, clay arrow keys, beads, card surfaces and decorative strokes are code-native CSS/SVG. They remain crisp at different viewport sizes. The sharing graphic is produced by the included Python source using the original logo, the generated camera and local fonts; it is not a browser screenshot.

## Fonts

Bricolage Grotesque (800), DM Sans (400 and 600). Fonts are served locally, with subset WOFF files for runtime and source TTF files for regeneration. SIL Open Font License notices are included as `Bricolage-Grotesque-OFL.txt` and `DM-Sans-OFL.txt`.
